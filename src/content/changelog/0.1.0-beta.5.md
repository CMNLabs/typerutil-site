- **Free 7-day trial.** Typer Util is free to try for 7 days, then needs a license. The menu bar menu and
  the palette show how many days are left.
- **License keys.** Buy a license from the palette, the menu bar menu or Preferences → License, then
  enter the key from your receipt email in Preferences → License. One license covers up to 5 Macs; use
  **Deactivate on This Mac** to move it to another.
- **Automatic updates.** Typer Util checks for new versions daily, or choose **Check for Updates…** in the
  menu bar menu. Turn automatic checks off in Preferences → General.
- Signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a security warning.
