**Typer Util 1.0** types text into apps and web forms that block pasting or mangle pasted text.

- Press **⇧⌘K** anywhere, enter your text, press **Ready** (⌘⏎), then click into the field. Typer Util
  types it once the countdown ends.
- **Slow, Normal and Fast** speeds (⌘1–⌘3), or set the exact time between keystrokes.
- **Warnings before surprises**: new lines and tabs (which press Return and Tab, and can submit forms),
  and characters that aren't on your keyboard layout. Each has an option to avoid it.
- **Works from anywhere**: the Services menu, a `typerutil://` URL scheme, AppleScript, and a `typerutil`
  command for Terminal.
- **Free for 7 days**, then one license covers up to 5 Macs.
- **Automatic updates**, signed and notarized by Apple.
